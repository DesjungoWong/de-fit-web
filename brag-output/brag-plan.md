# Brag Plan: Atrium & AISLE — Citizen Services Asset Catalogue

## What is this app?
A scroll-scrubbed "asset story" page: two 3D chapter stories for the lead platforms (Atrium, the connective layer for the built environment; AISLE, a reusable case backbone for law enforcement), then a light catalogue of 16 more reusable assets.

## The angle
Two worlds, one promise: you don't rip anything out. Atrium is lilac — six existing systems wired into one shared column, running on whatever AI you already use. A colour flood turns the stage aqua for AISLE — one case record with eight modules orbiting it and agents kept outside on governed lanes. Then the lights come up on the paper-white catalogue: the cards keep coming.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark aubergine ground. "PLUG, DON'T REPLACE." in condensed serif caps lands word by word; "don't" in lilac. The Atrium ring funnel glows behind it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Atrium: six system boxes (Case systems, Documents and archives, Rules engines / Shared platforms, Portals and email, Project records) light up and dashed flows run into the ATRIUM column; the AI card on top rolls Copilot → Anthropic → OpenAI → Your own.
- AISLE: the CASE 360 cuboid sits centre; eight module pills (Incident … Offence) pop onto the ring one by one; three lane labels Assisted / Supervised / Autonomous and an amber "APIs only" ring.
- Catalogue: asset cards slide into a grid on the beat (GENiE 3.0, META, AVA Voice Platform, GDC Air-gap Kit, AVA Holobox, GeBIZ Intelligence) with maturity pills.

## Outro / punchline
"Atrium and AISLE" big serif, under it "+ 16 assets we can reuse". Hold 2.5s+.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only (scroll story). The scroll-scrubbed chapter progression (rail fill + chapter kicker "ATRIUM · 04 / 06 · RUNTIME") stands in for the flow.

## Tone

- Creative direction: quiet premium product film, two colour worlds then daylight
- Interpretation: few scenes, long settled holds, soft blur swaps and one colour flood; no jokes; the diagrams do the work.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed under narration, sparse accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady, clean; polished)
- Music treatment: 0.32 normally; ducked to ~0.13 under each VO line; fade in 0.6s, fade out over last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read (109.96 BPM). Strong cues: 9.83/9.29 (Atrium→AISLE flood, lock seam at 9.83 beat), 17.47 (last catalogue card), 19.66 (outro title). Beat grid for cards: 15.84, 16.38, 16.93, 17.47 (+ two more on 18.02, 18.56 — cards are fast-in, then held as a set).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS breathes the Atrium column glow and the AISLE cube glow. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse (polished): soft impact on the hook, bong on the AI-card roll, soft impact on the colour flood, a light drop per module (first/last only), a card slide on the first catalogue card, a bell on the outro.
- Restraint rule: nothing over the narration's consonants; SFX ≤ 0.6.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.2s
"PLUG, DON'T REPLACE." per word blur-in; kicker "ATRIUM · 05 / 06 · INTEGRATION". Rings of the Atrium column fade up below.
Sequential/interaction: words one at a time (0.1s apart), then hold ~2s.
Audio intent: soft weight on arrival. Audio-coupled idea: impact on first word.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (headline blur-swap, stage stays).

### Scene 2 — Atrium — 6.6s
Diagram: six system boxes, dashed flows with travelling dots into the ATRIUM ring column; "one shared space". AI card above cycles Copilot / Anthropic / OpenAI / Your own (roll every ~0.9s). Headline "RUNS ON YOUR AI" ("your" lilac) + chips MCP · RAG. Side rail fills as progress.
Sequential/interaction: systems light up left/right in pairs; AI name rolls.
Audio intent: confident build. Audio-coupled idea: bong on first roll.
Transition mood: colour flood → Scene 3 (lock to beat 9.83).

### Scene 3 — AISLE — 5.8s
Ground floods navy. CASE 360 cuboid; eight module pills arrive around the ring one by one (80ms stagger); then three lanes labelled Assisted / Supervised / Autonomous and an amber "APIs only" ring. Headline "AGENTS BESIDE, NOT INSIDE" ("beside" aqua), chips "MCP gateway · OPA / Cedar policy".
Sequential/interaction: modules one by one (fast stagger, held as a set ≥3s).
Audio intent: steady. Audio-coupled: soft impact at flood, drop on first module.
Transition mood: light comes up → Scene 4 (ground brightens to paper).

### Scene 4 — Catalogue — 4.0s
Paper ground. Eyebrow "FULL CATALOGUE", serif "Everything else we can reuse". Six cards slide up on beats with art, name, subtitle, maturity pill (Production / Pilot / In development).
Sequential/interaction: cards on 15.84 → 18.56 beat grid, held together ≥1.5s.
Audio intent: light, tidy. Audio-coupled: card slide on first card.
Transition mood: cards blur back → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.0s
"Atrium and AISLE" big serif (lands 19.66, strong cue), "+ 16 assets we can reuse" in mono violet, small "Citizen Services Asset Catalogue". Hold to end.
Audio: bell on landing; music fades.
